## Primary sources and content boundaries

- [USCCB Luke 2](https://bible.usccb.org/bible/luke/2), read in full: birth/shepherd narrative, including Mary, Joseph, Bethlehem, Nazareth, manger, flock, angel and swaddling.
- [USCCB Matthew 2](https://bible.usccb.org/bible/matthew/2), read in full: Magi, star, gold, frankincense, myrrh and dream. The text describes a house and does not name or number the Magi.

These are identified Christian texts, not independent historical verification or universal beliefs. Original contextual paraphrases distinguish the accounts; they do not combine the Magi and shepherd visits into a single asserted chronology. No passage, commentary, prayer, hymn, copyrighted character or copied puzzle bank is reproduced. No age, grade, educational outcome or low-vision claim is made.

The sixteen answers are JESUS, MARY, JOSEPH, BETHLEHEM, NAZARETH, MANGER, SHEPHERDS, FLOCK, ANGEL, SWADDLING, MAGI, GOLD, FRANKINCENSE, MYRRH, STAR and DREAM. The seeded 16×16 medium configuration uses E/S/SE/SW paths; medium describes configuration rather than certified reading ability.

## X10 remains research only

Broad “Christmas Around the World” representation is not justified by a generic country checklist or one custom per nation. No X10 candidate is authored here. A possible smaller companion has primary-source leads from [University of Arizona Libraries' Tucson Las Posadas collection](https://lib.arizona.edu/special-collections/collections/las-posadas-collection) and [Nordiska museet's layered account of Lucia](https://www.nordiskamuseet.se/en/traditions-celebrations/lucia/). Those document particular communities and changing traditions, not universal national customs. Smithsonian family accounts were visible in search excerpts, but full-page access failed; they are not used to author a candidate. Cultural review, spelling/context checks and a distinct purpose remain necessary. Research is not implemented content or expert approval.
