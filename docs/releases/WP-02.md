# WP-02 — Difficulty and direction consistency

Base: PR #2 `release/2026-W46-direction-validation` at c6539992f95ea584498329bc319ac38edbbe160e. This branch is stacked for review and does not authorize deployment.

## Diagnosis and compatibility

The 150 published puzzle records comprise 48 Easy, 86 Medium and 16 Hard. Every published Medium uses all eight directions, while the generator Medium preset uses E, S, SE, NE. Every published Easy uses E, S, SE while the generator Easy preset uses E, S. Published Hard and generator Hard both use all eight. [The affected Medium URL inventory](WP-02-inventory.md) lists all 86 records. This is a comparison of allowed directions, not a claim that every direction occurs in every seeded grid. The curated collection descriptions describe the curated records, not the builder preset.

Before this change clicking the already-selected difficulty reapplied the generator preset and could therefore replace curated or user-customized directions, changing the grid and answer key. Now selecting the current difficulty keeps the selection; choosing another difficulty retains the existing preset behavior. The control explains this. Invalid-direction recovery explicitly applies the Medium preset, including when Medium is already selected. No generator behavior, published configuration, seeds, output serialization, or canonical routes change. Existing snapshots cover all 150 published full puzzle results and share paths.

Recommendation: keep published definitions and preset meanings stable until the owner chooses a single documented policy; consider explicitly distinguishing curated difficulty from builder presets, with migration and URL/seed compatibility review before changing either. The active-button fix avoids accidental replacement but does not resolve the differing definitions.
